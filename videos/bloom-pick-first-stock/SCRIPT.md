# SCRIPT — Bloom Lesson 1: Pick Your First Stock

**Target:** ~60 seconds, portrait 1080×1920, ElevenLabs v3 voice.
**Structure:** Hook → Reframe → Concept → Examples → Try-it → Reveal → Payoff → Insight → Breakdown → Bridge → Tease (11 beats)
**Word count:** ~120 spoken words
**VO direction:** Same trusted-older-sibling register as the Bloom promo. Confident, warm, conversational. Pauses at periods are intentional — the visual fills them.

---

## Beat 1 — Hook
> Picking your first stock can feel intimidating.

## Beat 2 — Reframe
> But you may already know great companies to invest in.

## Beat 3 — Concept
> Look around. The brands you use every day are often the ones worth owning.

## Beat 4 — Examples
> Who makes the phone in your hand? The shoes on your feet? The drink in your fridge?

## Beat 5 — Try it
> List five companies you interact with almost every day.

## Beat 6 — Reveal
> Maybe you thought of Apple. Starbucks. Nike. Or Netflix.

## Beat 7 — Payoff
> Every one of these — you can invest in, right on Bloom.

## Beat 8 — Insight
> Your insight as a customer is a superpower.

## Beat 9 — Breakdown
> You know which products people love, which brands stick, which companies are growing.

## Beat 10 — Bridge
> But finding companies is only step one.

## Beat 11 — Tease
> Next: how to tell a great company from a great investment.

Picking your first stock can feel intimidating. But you may already know great companies to invest in. Look around. The brands you use every day are often the ones worth owning. Every one of these — you can invest in, right on Bloom. Your insight as a customer is a superpower. You know which products people love, which brands stick, which companies are growing. But finding companies is only step one. Next: how to tell a great company from a great investment.
