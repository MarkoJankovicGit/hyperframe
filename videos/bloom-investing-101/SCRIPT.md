## Spoken text (saved as `narration.txt`):

Did you know you can make money while you're sleeping? Well, say hi to investing. Investing means buying anything that can increase in value over time. Like sneakers, houses, or stocks. It's hard to get rich by saving alone, because you only grow your money by working more or spending less. But by investing, your money can work for you and grow on its own. For example, if you save thirty dollars a month from age eighteen, you would have sixteen thousand dollars by age sixty-five. But invest that same thirty dollars a month into stocks from age eighteen, and it could become three hundred and eighty thousand dollars by age sixty-five. Assuming a ten percent annual return. Investing takes time, patience, and discipline. But it's the best way to build long-term wealth, and Bloom will teach you how to invest like a pro.
