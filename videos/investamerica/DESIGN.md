# Design System

## Overview

Bloom Invest America is a warm, editorial financial landing page built around cream surfaces, refined serif headlines, and vivid blue action accents. The page alternates spacious hero typography, product screenshots, chart panels, trust stats, and rounded white feature cards. The design feels optimistic and parent-friendly while still borrowing credibility from brokerage-style charts and institutional reporting. Visual motion should feel polished, calm, and purposeful rather than loud.

## Colors

- **Primary Surface**: `#F2EEE6` - warm cream canvas used across the hero and closing CTA.
- **Secondary Surface**: `#F8F6F2` - soft off-white section background for feature and proof areas.
- **Card Surface**: `#FFFFFF` - rounded cards, report panels, and CTA pills.
- **Ink**: `#212C33` - main serif headings and high-priority text.
- **Body Slate**: `#535F66` - explanatory copy and labels.
- **Bloom Blue**: `#1658FF` - CTAs, underlines, chart strokes, badges, and icon accents.
- **Deep Blue**: `#055AC0` - secondary blue for trust/protection iconography.
- **Navy Field**: `#0A1A63` - premium dark sections and copy-trading/product moments.
- **Quiet Border**: `#E8E2D6` - subtle grid lines, dividers, and card separation.
- **Soft Gray**: `#E5E7EB` - secondary outlines and inactive UI marks.

## Typography

- **Display Serif**: Instrument Serif, weights 400 and 600. Used for large emotional headlines, major stats, quote text, and the final CTA.
- **Interface Sans**: Inter, weights 300, 400, 500, 600, and 700. Used for body copy, badges, buttons, labels, disclosures, and product UI.
- **Sizing pattern**: Hero/site headings land around 60-96px on desktop. Feature cards use 20-24px headings. Body copy is calm and readable with generous line-height.

## Elevation

Depth is soft and minimal. Rounded white cards sit on cream backgrounds with subtle shadows and quiet borders. Product screenshots and report panels create stronger depth through large rounded rectangles, blue fields, and device-like frames rather than heavy glass or dramatic drop shadows.

## Components

- **Cream Editorial Hero**: centered serif headline, blue hand-drawn underline, pill proof badge, and floating sticker illustrations.
- **Growth Chart Panel**: blue line chart with soft filled area, dotted age markers, and stat cards underneath.
- **Step Timeline**: four numbered nodes connected by a thin blue line, paired with small icon-like stickers.
- **Copy-Trading Showcase**: deep blue feature field with investor rows, percentage badges, and a product/mockup card.
- **Investment Profile Report**: white report panel paired with a large blue explanatory panel and a rounded white CTA.
- **Feature Cards**: white rounded cards with pale blue icon chips and restrained body copy.
- **Trust Stat Wall**: large serif figures, muted company/news logos, and an italic quote.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Use `#F2EEE6` and `#F8F6F2` as the dominant surfaces.
- Let `#1658FF` carry motion, emphasis, chart drawing, and CTA energy.
- Use Instrument Serif for emotional financial headlines and big numbers.
- Use captured product screenshots, stickers, and chart SVGs as moving layers.
- Keep cards rounded, airy, and softly elevated.

### Don'ts

- Do not turn the whole piece into a dark fintech UI; dark blue is only an accent scene.
- Do not use generic stock imagery when captured product assets are available.
- Do not use harsh neon gradients or purple-blue washes.
- Do not make text-only beats; every scene needs product, chart, sticker, or UI motion.
- Do not overstate government affiliation; preserve the educational/product framing.
