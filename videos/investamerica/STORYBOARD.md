# Storyboard

**Format:** 1080x1920 portrait (9:16 TikTok/Reels)  
**Audio:** TTS voiceover if available; otherwise visual-only timing with room for narration.  
**VO direction:** warm, confident, parent-facing delivery; measured pacing with small pauses after each proof point.  
**Style basis:** DESIGN.md, captured Invest America landing page.

## BEAT 1 - HEAD START (0:00-0:06)

**VO:** "What if one thousand dollars at birth could become a real head start?"

**Concept:** The viewer lands inside Bloom's cream editorial hero. The headline feels human and aspirational, while small sticker elements drift like a child's financial future coming into focus.

**Visual:** Bloom logo anchors the top-left. A blue proof badge glides into the upper center. The serif headline builds as a large vertical lockup, with a hand-drawn blue underline animating beneath the payoff. Pink sticker assets float at different depths around the tall frame.

**Animation choreography:** Logo settles from soft blur. Badge slides up and sharpens. Headline words cascade upward. Underline draws left to right. Stickers float in slow opposing paths.

**Transition:** Blur-through into the growth chart.

## BEAT 2 - GROWTH (0:06-0:13)

**VO:** "Invest America gives every American child a Treasury-backed investment account. Bloom helps families understand it, track it, and build on it."

**Concept:** The page becomes a calm financial dashboard. Blue carries the eye through time, while stat cards convert the abstract account into real milestones.

**Visual:** The captured growth chart sits in a tall rounded white panel, cropped for portrait. Three stat cards stack underneath so each value is readable on mobile. A blue sheen travels across the chart while the values lock into place.

**Animation choreography:** Panel rises in. Chart image slowly pushes forward. Stat cards cascade in and their numbers sharpen into place. A small disclosure line fades in last.

**Transition:** Whip pan into the feature/product showcase.

## BEAT 3 - BLOOM LAYER (0:13-0:19)

**VO:** "See how that first one thousand can grow. Add optional contributions, learn with Bloomie A I, and even explore copy trading from top investors."

**Concept:** This is the product value beat. The calm cream page opens into a saturated Bloom-blue field with moving cards: learning, AI mentoring, parental controls, and copy trading.

**Visual:** Deep blue background. Feature cards stack at the top like TikTok overlay cards. The copy-trading mockup dominates the lower half, with a cropped site screenshot tucked behind it as a report layer.

**Animation choreography:** Blue field wipes in. Product mockup tilts gently and slowly zooms. Feature cards stagger into a vertical stack. White check chips pop in with restrained bounce.

**Transition:** Velocity-matched upward into trust proof.

## BEAT 4 - PROOF AND CTA (0:19-0:26)

**VO:** "Over four million children are already registered. With zero fees, parental controls, and personalized investing education, Bloom turns a government account into a family plan. Claim your child's account, then build their future with Bloom."

**Concept:** The final beat returns to the cream brand world and resolves into proof plus action. It should feel credible, calm, and immediate.

**Visual:** Four trust stats sit in a readable 2x2 grid at the top. The final serif line is rebuilt as native HTML: "The American Dream starts with $1,000." Bloom logo, blue spark accents, a hand-drawn underline, and a blue CTA pill sit below. The house sticker floats into the lower corner.

**Animation choreography:** Stats count and settle. Spark accents pop around the native headline. Final headline rises with a blue underline draw. CTA pill brightens once and holds.

**Transition:** Final gentle fade to cream.
