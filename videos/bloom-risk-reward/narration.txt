Think investing is just a gamble? It's actually a calculation of risk versus reward. Risk is how much you can lose. Reward is what you stand to gain. And every single investment has both. Whether it's the phone in your hand, the shoes on your feet, or a stock like Disney. Imagine you invest ten dollars in Disney. If the company goes out of business, your risk is ten dollars. But if the stock doubles, your reward is ten dollars. Your ten dollars becomes twenty. You control your risk by deciding how much to invest, and what to pick. Higher risk, higher reward. Lower risk, safer returns. You've finished your first step in understanding the relationship between risk and reward. Next: how to maximize your reward while minimizing your risk: diversification.
