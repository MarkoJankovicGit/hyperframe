# Bloom Diversification Script

Want the most popular way to lower risk while still chasing reward? It's called diversification. Imagine you invest one thousand dollars, all in Tesla. If Tesla fails, your whole one thousand dollars can disappear. A better move is spreading that money across five companies: two hundred dollars in each one. Now, if one company struggles, you still have four others working for you. That practice, spreading investments across different companies, industries, and asset classes, is diversification. It's the investing version of don't put all your eggs in one basket. Diversification reduces risk because different investments rarely all crash at the exact same time. If you only own tech stocks like Apple and Meta, a tech slump can pull your whole portfolio down. In twenty twenty two, Meta dropped nearly seventy percent. But if you also owned healthcare stocks, your portfolio had another engine. Moderna surged during the demand for COVID testing and vaccines. So diversification is not just about avoiding losses. It can also give you more ways to profit across different market conditions. You could ride the tech wave from twenty fourteen to twenty twenty one, then still benefit when healthcare rallied in twenty twenty two. Next lesson: the easiest way to diversify, ETFs.
