You can actually own a piece of Apple. A stock is a slice of ownership in a company. When you buy a stock, you become a part-owner. You can buy stocks of companies you know like Apple or Nike. As a part-owner, you hope your company grows so you grow your money alongside it. But how does your money actually grow? Every stock has a stock price, representing the value of a single share. Stock prices go up and down every day. So your investment can also change every day — sometimes by a lot! If your stock's price goes up, your investment increases in value. Stocks are one of the best long-term investments, having grown on average ten percent per year over the last 30 years. Bloom will teach you how to evaluate and choose stocks to start your investing journey.
