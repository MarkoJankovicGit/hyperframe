# SCRIPT — Bloom Promotional Reel (15s)

**Target:** 15 seconds, portrait 1080×1920, ElevenLabs voice.
**Structure:** Hook → Feature → Proof → CTA
**Word count:** 34 spoken words (~13.6s pure speech + pauses between beats ≈ 15s).
**VO direction:** Warm, energetic, confident — Gen Z register. Think "trusted older sibling who just figured this out and wants you in on it." Short sentences, clear pauses between beats. Contractions natural.

---

## Beat 1 — Hook (≈0.0 → 3.6s)

> Wall Street shouldn't feel like a secret club.

*Why this hook:* Sets up a contrast — the problem (gatekept finance) before the product. Avoids the generic "Introducing Bloom" opener. Provokes agreement from a Gen Z viewer who has felt locked out of investing.

## Beat 2 — Feature (≈3.8 → 7.8s)

> Bloom turns it into bite-size lessons. Real stocks. Zero jargon.

*Source copy:* Site headline *"Finally, Stock Market Education That Actually Makes Sense"* plus subhead *"No confusing jargon. No boring textbooks. Just clear, bite-sized lessons."* Condensed.

## Beat 3 — Proof (≈8.0 → 12.0s)

> Two million downloads. Money protected up to five hundred thousand.

*Source copy:* Footer stats *"2 Million+ Downloads"* and *"SIPC insured up to $500K"*. The SIPC abbreviation is deliberately spelled as the protected-amount plain-English phrasing so TTS reads it naturally.

## Beat 4 — CTA (≈12.2 → 14.7s)

> Learn. Invest. Automate. Get Bloom.

*Source copy:* Direct from hero h1 *"Learn. Invest. Automate."* + branded close.

## Pronunciation Notes

- "Bloom" — pronounced naturally; no substitution.
- "Wall Street" — natural.

- "Five hundred thousand" — spelled out (don't send `$500K`).
