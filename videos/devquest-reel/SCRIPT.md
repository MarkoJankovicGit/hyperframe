# DevQuest — Narration Script (English)

Target: ≤60s @ ~2.2–2.4 words/sec → ~110 words. Portrait reel presenting the platform to educational institutions, enterprises and course creators.

---

## Beats

**Beat 1 — Hook (0:00–0:05)**
What if learning felt... like a game?

**Beat 2 — What is DevQuest (0:05–0:12)**
DevQuest turns knowledge into an adventure. No boring lectures — every lesson is a quest.

**Beat 3 — World of knowledge (0:12–0:19)**
Every subject is a world. Every topic — a dungeon to conquer.

**Beat 4 — Student use case (0:19–0:29)**
Students learn through five-minute challenges. Code. Quiz. Correct answer — XP, streaks, level up.

**Beat 5 — RPG progression (0:29–0:36)**
Motivation is built in: ranks, badges, and progress you can see.

**Beat 6 — Professors & institutions (0:36–0:45)**
Professors turn their curriculum into quests — and track every student's progress in real time.

**Beat 7 — Enterprise & creators (0:45–0:53)**
The same magic works beyond the classroom. Corporate training. Team onboarding. Creator courses.

**Beat 8 — CTA (0:53–0:59)**
DevQuest. Learn by playing. Start your quest.

---

Total: ~105 words → fits comfortably in 59s with pauses and visual breathing room.
