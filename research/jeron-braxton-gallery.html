Style Reference · Buffett Indicator v2

Jeron Braxton

Self-taught NYC animator (b. 1995). Sundance Jury Award for Glucose (2017). Known for low-poly, PS1/PS2-era 3D aesthetic: blocky avatars, flat saturated color, intentional texture flicker and glitch, dreamlike spatial logic, religious/political imagery, neon-on-near-black palettes, and surreal CGI that fuses video-game vocabulary with Black-American narrative.

What we'll borrow for v2

  • Low-poly 3D objects instead of vector charts — a chunky Buffett bust, polygonal $ bricks, blocky vault
  • Saturated flat color blocks + dithered/glitch textures — Bloom blue stays the accent but goes neon
  • PS1 jitter — slight vertex wobble, no-AA edges, low-res texture mapping
  • Dreamlike camera moves — slow orbits, low-FOV flythroughs, repeating geometry
  • Bold typographic stamps — chunky sans + grain, overlaid on the 3D world (not floating UI)
  • Surreal scale jumps — Buffett-as-monument, cash-as-landscape, indicator-as-architecture
